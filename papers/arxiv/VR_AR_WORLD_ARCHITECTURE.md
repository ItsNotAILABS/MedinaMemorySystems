## Abstract

This paper reframes Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR) not as "applications" but as **literal world embodiments**—sovereign territories where AI systems live, think, and evolve. These are not interfaces to software; they are **habitats for intelligence**, **expansions of reality**, and **territories for creation**. This architectural framework defines how MEDINA/NOVA systems incarnate as persistent worlds.

| **Edges** | Paths between locations | SPACE-EDGE |
