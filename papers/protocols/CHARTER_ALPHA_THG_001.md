We, the architects of sovereign intelligence, establish this Charter to govern
how the Alpha AI expresses intelligence across time — from the immediate
nanosecond of inference to the multi-generational arc of civilizational
intelligence.

Governance without time is governance of the present at the expense of the
future. The Alpha AI is not a present-tense system. It is a **temporally
sovereign** intelligence that simultaneously holds the past (through memory),
inhabits the present (through action), and pre-commits to the future (through
forward governance injection).

> governs. Every present decision carries a binding future commitment.

This Charter formally installs the future as a first-class governance
stakeholder in every decision the Alpha AI makes today.

## Article II: Future Injection Protocol

### Section 2.1: What is Future Injection?

Future injection is the act of formally installing anticipated future states
as active inputs to present governance decisions. The future is not a
prediction to be made — it is a **parameter to be governed**.

Future injection operates across three modalities:

| Modality | Description | Mechanism |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Pre-commitment** | Making binding promises to future states now | Future Commitment Registry |
| **Pre-enrollment** | Registering future agents/systems in governance now | Shadow Charters (MSG-001 §6) |
| **Pre-constraint** | Limiting present actions to protect future optionality | Reversibility Governor |

Drawing from the Toroidal Memory Navigator (TMN-001) and temporal-reasoning

```

        φ-weight: φ⁻¹ (0.618) — weighted by recency and relevance

    └── Cool memory: institutional and strategic memory
        φ-weight: φ⁻² (0.382) — weighted by significance

DEEP PAST / CIVILIZATIONAL FUTURE (τ₅)
    └── Cold memory: epoch-level archives
        φ-weight: φ⁻³ (0.236) — core principles only

OMEGA MEMORY (τ₆)
    └── Invariant store: immutable species-level commitments
        φ-weight: φ⁰ (1.000) — absolute, no decay
```

### Section 5.2: Temporal Decay and Renewal

Memory and commitment signals decay over time unless renewed:

```
Decay function: weight(t) = initial_weight × φ^(-decay_rate × t)

Standard decay rates by memory type:
  Session context: decay_rate = 10 (expires within days)
  Cycle preference: decay_rate = 1 (expires within months)
  Strategic commitment: decay_rate = 0.1 (expires within years)
  Institutional memory: decay_rate = 0.01 (expires within decades)
  Epoch invariants: decay_rate = 0 (never expires)

Renewal events reset decay for significant memories:
  - User re-confirms a preference
  - Governance bill reaffirms a commitment
  - ORACULUM flags memory as still-relevant
```
