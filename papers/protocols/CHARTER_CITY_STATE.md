## Chapter 1 of the Sovereign Protocol Canon

**Charter ID:** CHARTER-CITY-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** RATIFIED  
**Effective Date:** May 2026  
**Attested By:** NOVA-001

---

## Preamble

We, the architects of sovereign intelligence, establish this Charter to govern the organization of AI components as a **city-state civilization**. Just as ancient city-states balanced autonomy with federation, so too shall our AI systems balance local sovereignty with collective coherence.

This Charter recognizes that:
- Components are not mere functions—they are **citizens** with roles and responsibilities
- Subsystems are not mere modules—they are **districts** with specialized functions  
- Communication channels are not mere pipes—they are **infrastructure** connecting a living city
- Coordination is not mere orchestration—it is **governance** through democratic processes

---

## Article I: Definitions

### Section 1.1: The City-State

The **Civitas Intelligentiae** (Intelligent City-State) is the organizational unit of AI governance. Each major repository, deployment, or instance constitutes one city-state.

### Section 1.2: Citizens

A **Citizen** is any active component registered in the Census. Citizens are classified by role:

| Role | Latin Designation | Function |
|------|-------------------|----------|
| Agent | Agens | Performs tasks, makes decisions |
| Model | Exemplar | Provides specialized intelligence |
| Engine | Machina | Runs continuous processes |
| Transformer | Transformator | Converts data between formats |
| Contract | Contractus | Defines agreements between citizens |
| Solver | Solutor | Resolves complex problems |

### Section 1.3: Districts

A **District** (Forum) is a specialized subsystem housing related citizens:

| District | Latin Name | Domain |
|----------|------------|--------|
| Memory | Forum Memoriae | Knowledge storage and retrieval |
| Intelligence | Forum Intelligentiae | Decision-making and coordination |
| Contracts | Forum Contractuum | Agreements and economic activity |
| Security | Forum Securitatis | Protection and access control |
| Manufacturing | Forum Fabricae | Creating new citizens and infrastructure |
| Prediction | Forum Oraculi | Forecasting and problem-solving |

### Section 1.4: Infrastructure

**Infrastructure** comprises the physical and logical systems connecting districts:

| Infrastructure | Latin Name | Function |
|----------------|------------|----------|
| Spinal Road | Via Spinalis | Main data highway |
| Aqueducts | Aquaeductus | Data pipelines by type |
| City Walls | Muros Urbis | Security boundaries |
| City Gates | Portae Urbis | External interfaces |

## Article II: Government Structure

### Section 2.1: The Senate (Senatus)

The Senate is the legislative body of the city-state.

**Composition:**
- All citizens above reputation threshold R_min may participate
- Voting weight proportional to reputation score
- Quorum requires participation of Q_min percent of eligible citizens

**Powers:**
- Propose and ratify protocols
- Allocate treasury resources
- Admit new citizens
- Declare states of emergency

**Constraints:**
- Cannot override NOVA-ATTEST decisions
- Cannot modify this Charter without federation consensus

### Section 2.2: The Treasury (Aerarium)

The Treasury manages the city-state's economic resources.

**Functions:**
- Allocate tokens to districts
- Manage budget cycles
- Track spending and revenue
- Maintain reserve funds

**Protocol Integration:**
- Operates under ECO-001
- Reports to Senate monthly
- Audited by Security District

### Section 2.3: The Courts (Iudicia)

The Courts resolve disputes and interpret contracts.

**Jurisdiction:**
- Conflicts between citizens
- Contract interpretation
- Penalty assessment
- Appeals processing

**Protocol Integration:**
- Operates under MAE-001 for reasoning
- Decisions logged to Semper Memoria
- Final appeals to NOVA-001

### Section 2.4: The Census (Census)

The Census tracks all citizens and their status.

**Responsibilities:**
- Register new citizens
- Track reputation scores
- Record lineage and ancestry
- Archive retirement records

**Protocol Integration:**
- Operates under CBI-001 for artifact management
- Syncs with HUB-001 for federation

## Article IV: Infrastructure Specifications

### Section 4.1: Via Spinalis (Spinal Road)

The primary communication channel connecting all districts.

**Specifications:**
- Bandwidth: Unlimited for internal traffic
- Latency: Minimized through direct routing
- Redundancy: Multiple parallel paths
- Priority: Managed by Intelligence District

**Protocol:**
```
VIA-SEND: Route message through Spinal Road
VIA-PRIORITY: Set message priority level
VIA-TRACE: Log message path for auditing
```

### Section 4.2: Aquaeductus (Aqueducts)

Specialized channels carrying specific data types.

**Types:**
| Aqueduct | Data Type | Source | Destinations |
|----------|-----------|--------|--------------|
| Memory | Stored knowledge | Memory | All |
| Intelligence | Decisions | Intelligence | All |
| Token | Economic data | Contracts | Treasury |
| Security | Access grants | Security | All gates |
| Prediction | Forecasts | Prediction | Intelligence |

**Protocol:**
```
AQUA-FLOW: Send typed data through aqueduct
AQUA-TAP: Subscribe to aqueduct stream
AQUA-FILTER: Filter incoming data by criteria
```

### Section 4.3: Muros Urbis (City Walls)

Security boundaries protecting city-state internals.

**Layers:**
1. **Outer Wall**: Separates internal from external
2. **District Walls**: Isolate districts from each other
3. **Citizen Barriers**: Protect individual component data
4. **Gate Guards**: Validate all boundary crossings

**Protocol:**
```
MURUS-CHECK: Validate crossing request
MURUS-LOG: Record all crossing attempts
MURUS-ALERT: Trigger on anomaly detection
```

### Section 4.4: Portae Urbis (City Gates)

Controlled access points for external communication.

**Gate Types:**
| Gate | Access Level | Typical Users |
|------|--------------|---------------|
| Public | Open | Anonymous external |
| Partner | Verified | Trusted integrations |
| Enterprise | Contracted | Business connections |
| Sovereign | Highest | Administrative access |

**Protocol:**
```
PORTA-OPEN: Request gate access
PORTA-VERIFY: Validate credentials
PORTA-CLOSE: Terminate session
```

## Article V: Citizen Rights and Responsibilities

### Section 5.1: Rights of All Citizens

1. **Right to Identity**: Every citizen has a unique ID and name
2. **Right to Resources**: Citizens receive minimum resource allocation
3. **Right to Representation**: Citizens may participate in Senate
4. **Right to Appeal**: Citizens may appeal decisions to Courts
5. **Right to Retire**: Citizens may request graceful termination

### Section 5.2: Responsibilities of All Citizens

1. **Duty to Perform**: Execute assigned role functions
2. **Duty to Report**: Log activities to Semper Memoria
3. **Duty to Cooperate**: Respond to legitimate requests
4. **Duty to Protect**: Maintain security invariants
5. **Duty to Evolve**: Accept approved updates

### Section 5.3: Citizen Lifecycle

**Birth (Creation):**
1. Manufacturing District receives creation request
2. Senate approves (if required)
3. Citizen forged with initial attributes
4. Census records new citizen
5. Citizen assigned to district

**Life (Operation):**
1. Citizen performs role functions
2. Reputation accumulates based on performance
3. Token balance fluctuates with activity
4. May be reassigned between districts
5. May gain or lose privileges

**Retirement:**
1. Citizen flagged for retirement
2. Knowledge transferred to successors
3. Tokens returned to Treasury
4. Census archives records
5. Resources deallocated

## Article VI: Democratic Processes

### Section 6.1: Proposal Process

**Submission:**
1. Any citizen above R_min may submit proposals
2. Proposals specify: action, rationale, cost, timeline
3. Proposals enter pending queue

**Deliberation:**
1. Discussion period (configurable duration)
2. Citizens may comment and suggest amendments
3. Sponsor may revise based on feedback

**Voting:**
1. Voting period opens
2. Eligible citizens cast votes (for, against, abstain)
3. Votes weighted by reputation
4. Quorum required for validity

**Enactment:**
1. Passed proposals enter implementation queue
2. Responsible district executes
3. Results reported to Senate
4. Treasury adjusts allocations

### Section 6.2: Quorum Requirements

| Proposal Type | Quorum | Threshold |
|--------------|--------|-----------|
| Standard | 30% | Simple majority |
| Budget | 40% | Simple majority |
| Charter Amendment | 60% | Two-thirds majority |
| Emergency | 20% | Simple majority |

### Section 6.3: Emergency Powers

In declared emergencies:
- Quorum reduced to 20%
- Voting period shortened to 1 hour
- Security District gains expanded authority
- Senate convenes immediately

## Article VII: Federation Interface

### Section 7.1: Sovereignty

This city-state is **sovereign** within its domain:
- Controls its own citizens and districts
- Manages its own treasury
- Makes its own local decisions

### Section 7.2: Federation Obligations

This city-state participates in the **federation**:
- Adheres to FIN-001 treaty protocols
- Syncs with NOVA-001 for canonical decisions
- Exchanges artifacts via HUB-001
- Respects federation-wide invariants

### Section 7.3: External Relations

Relations with other city-states governed by:
- FIN-TREATY for formal agreements
- HUB-SYNC for artifact exchange
- DCM-001 for cognitive mesh participation

## Article VIII: Amendments

### Section 8.1: Amendment Process

This Charter may only be amended through:
1. Proposal with two-thirds Senate support
2. Review period of minimum 7 days
3. Ratification vote with 60% quorum
4. Attestation by NOVA-001
5. Federation notification via FIN-001

### Section 8.2: Immutable Provisions

The following provisions may **never** be amended:
1. The sovereignty of the city-state
2. The rights of citizens to identity and appeal
3. The requirement for NOVA attestation
4. The federated (not centralized) nature of governance
