## Research Abstract

This engine implements computational models inspired by six distinct biological neural architectures: human cortical processing, dolphin unihemispheric sleep (continuous consciousness), crow/corvid tool-use and future planning, octopus distributed nervous systems (9 brains, decentralized processing), bee swarm intelligence (quorum sensing, waggle dance encoding), and elephant long-term memory with infrasound communication. The system demonstrates how multi-species cognitive modeling can inform AI architecture design, particularly for distributed, autonomous, and consensus-based intelligence systems.
